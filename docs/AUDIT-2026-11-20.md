# 721 Deals — Full Pre-Launch Audit

**Date:** Fri 2026-11-20, 01:05–01:10 AST
**Target:** Lovable preview — https://id-preview--28c4a4dc-3e5c-488d-8aa2-0982b63ae9e8.lovable.app
**Method:** Live browser audit, desktop 1919px + mobile ~500px, full flow (welcome → homepage → filters → cart → WhatsApp). Read-only, nothing changed. Second pass: verified the three headline findings against the Lovable project source (LanguageGate.tsx, DealTile.tsx, FlashClock.tsx, BentoMotion.tsx, i18n.tsx, styles.css).
**Verdict: NOT launch-ready yet.** 2 real blockers, 9 fixes, 6 polish items. No launch, no domain connect until the blockers are fixed.

## Correction from the first pass (important)

The audit first flagged the welcome screen as **missing entirely**. That was wrong. The welcome gate exists and works — it is a **once-per-browser gate**: `LanguageGate` renders only when no language is stored, and the choice is saved in `localStorage`. The 01:02 check saw it (first visit, picked English); the 01:05 audit ran in the same browser profile, so the gate was already dismissed. **Not a bug, not a blocker.** One real gap remains: once dismissed there is no way to re-open the gate from the header — the header toggle is language codes only, no flags (polish, item P6).

## Blockers

**B1 — All Tech deals wear both LIVE PARTNER and SAMPLE badges.**
All four All Tech products (Smart TV 55" $699→$499, Security camera $89→$59, Colour laser printer $329→$229, Refurbished smartphone $399→$269) show "LIVE PARTNER" and "SAMPLE" side by side in Top deals / All deals. Code confirms both badges render independently (`DealTile.tsx`: `is_live_partner` and `is_sample` are separate flags). A live partner must never read as sample stock. Fix: set `is_sample: false` on live-partner deals (or suppress SAMPLE when the store is a live partner). Keep the honest photo caveat on carousel slide 4 ("Sample photo — not confirmed product stock") until real All Tech photos are in.

**B2 — Countdown and cart are blank on first paint.**
On three fresh loads the countdown showed `--:--:--` in all three places (dark FLASH SALE band, teal band, carousel) and the cart showed 0; it populated after ~6 seconds and then ticked correctly. Code confirms the cause (`FlashClock.tsx`: `{hydrated ? formatCountdown(...) : "--:--:--"}` — the countdown is gated on hydration). A first-time visitor sees a dead countdown on a flash-sale page. Fix: render the countdown from the first paint instead of gating on hydration.

## Fixes

**F1 — Reserve outside flash hours silently charges the regular price.**
At 01:08 AST (outside 12–2pm / 7–9pm) Reserve on the Plain cotton tee ($25, flash $12) added $25 to the cart; only a small cart note says prices update live. The WhatsApp Reserve message also quotes $25, not the $12 flash price. Fix: warn on the product card before reserving outside flash hours ("Flash price $12 applies 12–2pm & 7–9pm — reserving now at $25"), and make the WhatsApp message say which price applies.

**F2 — "Big discounts" filter includes a 0%-off item.**
Crochet poncho top ($45, no flash price, no % badge) appears under "Big discounts". Fix: only items with a real flash discount belong there.

**F3 — "Under $10" filter can never match anything.**
Lowest flash price on the site is $12, lowest regular price $25. Its empty state is just "Nothing matches that search." with no clear-filter action. Fix: drop the filter or re-label it ("Under $15"), and add a clear-filters action to empty states.

**F4 — Two items in the flash-sale grids have no deal at all.**
Crochet poncho top $45 and Tin robot collection $40 (sold out) show no flash price and no % OFF badge in Top deals and All deals, despite sitting in a flash-sale grid. Fix: give them a flash price or move them out of the flash grids.

**F5 — Graphic print tee size "L" renders tiny.**
On mobile the "L" size button renders at ~a third the size of M/XL, like a clipped glyph (other sold-out sizes render at normal size). Fix: same sizing for all size buttons.

**F6 — Papiamento typo + missing diacritics.**
"Clothes" renders "Pańa" (Polish ń) in the nav and bento tile — confirmed in code (`i18n.tsx` line 493). Language names also lack diacritics in all languages: "Espanol", "Francais", "Kreyol Ayisyen". Fix: "Paña", "Español", "Français", "Kreyòl Ayisyen".

**F7 — "See more" (Top stores) does nothing.**
It only jumps to #store-grid — still 5 stores, no additional stores appear. Store cards don't open a store page either; they just run a search. Fix: either load more stores / open a store page, or remove the button.

**F8 — Bento hero size button inconsistency.**
In "Top deal right now" the sold-out M button reads "M · 0", while the same product elsewhere just reads "M" (sold out). Fix: one format everywhere.

**F9 — Teal-band "Up to 52% OFF" badge changes colour across captures.**
Coral in some captures, muted grey-tan with dark-teal text and noticeably lower contrast in others. If it cycles, the grey phase is hard to read. Fix: lock it to one high-contrast colour.

## Polish

**P1 — Bento glow is invisible in practice.**
The glow exists in code (`styles.css`: `bento-glow` keyframes cycling teal/coral/sun/ink over 16s) but the colours are 24–42% transparent on a pale-yellow background — over ~10 minutes of watching, the tiles showed only soft shadows. Fix: raise the opacity/spread so the glow actually reads.

**P2 — Head-cropped photography.**
The Plain cotton tee and 5-Pack Cotton Tees photos crop the model's head at the chin/neck in every card and in the large bento hero — a headless torso. Images are otherwise crisp, not blurry. Fix: re-crop or swap the photos.

**P3 — "Edit with Lovable" preview badge overlaps content.**
Bottom-right badge (with Dismiss) overlaps prices, Reserve buttons and headings on almost every view (e.g. Mystery Dress Box "Flash price $85", Samsung TV "Flash price $549"). Preview-only, but confirm it will not ship on the live domain.

**P4 — Carousel pause button isolated on mobile.**
Sits at the far right edge with a large gap from the Prev/dots/Next group. Fix: group the controls together.

**P5 — Sticky header + two-row yellow nav eat ~a third of the mobile viewport**
and frequently cover the tops of cards while scrolling. Fix: slim the mobile header/nav or unstick one of them.

**P6 — Small copy/UX nits.**
Search for "sneaker" reports "1 deals" (should be "1 deal"); "See more" (Top deals) expands 8→13 items but its label never changes to "See less"; the countdown is repeated three times on the homepage; the Samsung TV photo is reused for carousel slides 2 and 4 and shows UK streaming apps (BBC iPlayer, ITV Hub, My5) with white All Tech text over the busy image; the Running sneaker photo shows a Nike swoosh on a SAMPLE product; the homepage footer has no About / How it works / terms links (only WhatsApp, For stores, ©).
