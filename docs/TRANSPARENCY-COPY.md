## 1. How the money works

> **How the money works**
>
> You pay 721 Deals. Not the store. One payment, by card, on 721deals.com.
>
> We hold your money until you have the goods in your hands. Then we take our cut, hold a small safety reserve, and send the store the rest.
>
> That is the whole deal, in five steps:
>
> **1. You pay.** You pay the price on the listing, plus the delivery fee if you choose delivery. The full total is shown before you pay. Nothing is added after checkout.
>
> **2. We hold the money.** Your payment sits in a payment hold until your pickup or delivery is confirmed. It is the store's money, waiting. We never spend it on running the site.
>
> **3. We take our cut.** Starter stores pay 10% of the sale price. Pro stores pay 6%. The card fee comes off first, before the split. The exact cut for your order is shown in the seller dashboard before the store lists the item, and it cannot change for an order you have already paid for.
>
> **4. We hold a small reserve.** A safety buffer against refunds and chargebacks. It is delayed cash, not a fee. It comes back to the store — see "When you get paid" below.
>
> **5. We pay the store.** Every two weeks, after the hold clears. We cover the transfer fee. Balances under $100 roll to the next payout — we never pay $6.11 to send $40.
>
> 721 Deals is the middleman only. We do not own the stock. The sale is between you and the named store on the listing.

## 3. When you get paid

> **When you get paid**
>
> **The hold.** After your pickup or delivery is confirmed, the money is held for 48 hours if you are an established seller, or 7 days if you are a new seller — your first 30 days or your first 10 sales, whichever is later. [CORRECTED 2026-11-21, 5-pass audit: the first version said "first 10 sales" only.] The hold starts at confirmed handover, never at payment. A complaint freezes that one order only — never your whole balance.
>
> **Release is not payout.** The 48 hours or 7 days is when your money becomes releasable. You are actually paid on the fortnightly payout run, and only if at least 10 days have passed since the sale.
>
> **New businesses (Tier B):** your first payout also waits until your licence file is complete. We will tell you where your file stands — ask us any time.
>
> **Currency.** Prices on the site are shown in Caribbean guilders (Cg) first, with US dollars alongside (USD 1 = Cg 1.79). The figures on this page are in US dollars unless marked Cg.
>
> **The 10-day minimum.** Nothing reaches a seller faster than 10 days after the sale. That is the minimum by design, even if the hold has already cleared.
>
> **The payout cycle.** Payouts run every two weeks (14 days). Your payout statement shows, every time: gross sales, card fee, our cut, reserve held, reserve released, refunds, and net paid. You should never have to ask what was deducted.
>
> **How long it really takes, sale to bank:**
>
> - **Typical: 14–20 days.**
> - **Best case: 13–14 days.**
> - **Worst case: about 35 days** — if your sale lands just after a payout run and the bank transfer takes its full published time.
>
> **The first payout is slower.** The first payout from a new Stripe account takes 7–14 days, so the very first payout cycle will be slower than the ones after it. We are telling you now so it is not a surprise.
>
> **The $100 minimum.** If your payout balance is under $100, it rolls to the next payout run. We never pay a $6.11 transfer fee to send $40.
>
> **Three ways to receive your payout:**
>
> 1. **To your own Wise USD balance.** Wise-to-Wise, USD to USD, is free and instant at the payout run — never before the hold clears. You open your own Wise account and give us your USD details. We cannot issue a Wise card to your store from our account.
> 2. **To a Wise account belonging to someone in your store's management.** You name that person in writing to us first. The hold, the reserve and the payout cycle do not change — only the last mile does. (This option is waiting on a legal check — we will tell you when it is open.)
> 3. **By bank transfer to your Republic Bank or WIB account**, on the fortnightly run. We cover the $6.11 transfer fee.
>
> **What is still being confirmed.** Stripe is still in test mode, the payout account is not yet connected, and the Wise Business USD details are not yet pulled. No payout timeline is real until those three are done. **UNVERIFIED — owner: CEO (only he can sign in and submit).** The $6.11 Wise fee is confirmed from real Wise transfers; the outbound fee to a Sint Maarten bank is confirmed on the first real payout. **UNVERIFIED — owner: CEO, by doing the first big batch payout.**

## 4. What protects buyers

> **What protects buyers**
>
> - **Your money is held until you have the goods.** You pay 721 Deals, not the store. The money sits in a payment hold until your pickup or delivery is confirmed. The store is not paid before that.
> - **One QR pass, one pickup.** After you pay, you get a single-use QR pickup pass. The store scans it and checks the last 4 digits of your phone number. The QR alone never releases goods. The last 4 digits alone never release goods. Once scanned, the pass shows as used — a second scan, a screenshot, or a different clerk gets nothing.
> - **48 hours to speak up.** If the goods are wrong, damaged, or not as described, tell us within 48 hours of pickup or delivery, with a photo. That freezes the payout on that order so we can sort it out.
> - **A decision within 72 hours.** Simple disputes are decided within 72 hours of complete evidence: refund, partial refund, or dispute declined — with reasons shown to both sides.
> - **14-day cooling-off.** As our promise to you: if you bought online, you can cancel within 14 days after pickup or delivery, without giving a reason, and get a full refund including the standard delivery fee — provided the goods come back unused and in their original packaging. Return cost is yours, unless the return is because the goods were defective or not as described.
> - **If it is not as described, repair or replacement comes first.** If the goods are defective, not as described, or the wrong item, you may first ask for repair or replacement, free of charge. If that is impossible, refused, or not done in time, you may ask for a price reduction or a full refund on return of the goods.
> - **A complaint freezes one order only.** Your dispute never freezes another buyer's order or a store's whole balance.
> - **We check the stores.** Every store is verified in person — ID checked, licence and Chamber registration checked, premises visited — before it sells. The first listings from every store are checked by hand, photo against price.
> - **What we do not promise.** We are not the seller. The sale is between you and the named store. We do not laboratory-test goods, and the seller warrants authenticity and safety. We promise the process above: verified sellers, a payment hold, a QR-checked handover, and a time-limited dispute process.

## 5. What protects sellers

> **What protects sellers**
>
> - **You are paid on schedule, in writing.** Every two weeks, with a statement that shows gross, card fee, cut, reserve held, reserve released, refunds, and net — every time.
> - **The reserve is delayed cash, not a fee.** The 5% permanent reserve comes back after 30 days. The new-seller 10% comes back after 14 days. Both are shown in your dashboard at all times.
> - **A dispute freezes one order only.** One complaint never freezes your whole balance or your other payouts.
> - **The buyer has 48 hours to report a problem.** After that window, the payout moves on schedule. Late claims still follow the normal complaint process, but they do not hold your money hostage.
> - **Chargebacks are charged to the party at fault.** If a buyer's bank reverses a payment, the party at fault for that order bears the fee — $15, or $30 if contested and lost.
> - **No off-platform diversion against you.** A buyer may not use a 721 Deals order to move a sale off the platform, and neither may a seller. The rules cut both ways.
> - **Your rate cannot change under you.** The cut shown in your dashboard before you list is the cut for that order. It cannot change for an order already paid.
> - **Verification protects honest stores.** Every store is verified the same way you were. Stores that sell counterfeit goods, lie about prices, or fail to hand over paid goods are warned, paused, then removed — third upheld dispute means removal.
> - **What we need from you.** Real photos of the actual goods. Honest was-prices with proof. Accurate sizes, colours and stock. Goods held for the pickup window. A QR scan at every handover — never hand over on a last-4-digits check alone.

## 10. Do not publish — banned words and claims

Never use these words or claims in any 721 Deals copy — website, FAQs, seller onboarding, demo, advertising, or social posts:

- **"Escrow"** — never. The approved word is **"payment hold"** only.
- **"Insurance" / "insured"** — never. We hold money; we do not insure anything.
- **"Guaranteed" / "guarantee" / "buyer protection guarantee"** — never. We promise a process (hold, QR check, 48-hour window, 72-hour decision), not an outcome.
- **"Trust account"** — never.
- **"SXM-registered" / "registered in Sint Maarten"** — never, until the branch registration is actually done.
- **"Nothing is overseas"** — never, in the legal or banking sense. Payments run through our US company account because Stripe does not serve Sint Maarten businesses directly. Say that instead.
- **Any sales promise** — never promise a store a number of sales, orders, or revenue.
- **Any reach figure** — never quote a buyer-pool, audience, or follower number unless it is real, dated, and provable. No fake follower counts.
- **Any payout faster than the hold allows** — never promise payment in less than 10 days after the sale. Never promise "same-day" or "next-day" payouts.
- **"Final sale" / "as-is"** — never as a way to remove buyer rights. Conformity and cooling-off rights apply.
- **Any fee, rate, date, handle, or promise not in this file or the source documents** — never invent one.
- **Sample/mock stores, items, or prices presented as live partners** — everything that is not All Tech Electronics stays labelled SAMPLE.
- **"No-cut first 30 days"** — not decided. The founding offer is 30 days subscription-free with the 10% cut still applying. Do not advertise a cut-free month.
