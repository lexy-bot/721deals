## 1. The pitch in one line
**SXMDeals takes the cake — Dead stock. Real discounts. Pickup today.**
SXM stores overbought. We sell their leftover stock cheaper, in flash windows, pickup same day. No shipping wait, no Temu gamble, the store is down the road.

## 2. What we are NOT
- Not Amazon/Temu (no shipping, no 3-week wait, no fake "worth $500").
- Not Facebook Marketplace (no haggling, no no-shows with no money held, prices are real and revert after the flash).
- Not a warehouse: v1 is middleman only. Buyer pays SXMDeals, money held at Stripe until pickup/delivery confirmed. Never call the hold "escrow" or "insurance" without a lawyer.

## 10. Referral + games loop (the "giants aren't doing this" edge)
The giants do spin wheels. SXMDeals' edge is local + physical: the game ends at a real store, same day.
- **Treasure hunt → browsing:** 3 hidden badges on 3 real deal pages each morning → buyers browse slow dead stock to find them. First 20 finders/day win 10% off. Pure skill, no lottery issue.
- **Check-in → habit:** 5 coins/day, streak bonus, 100 coins = $1 off (max 30%, 90-day expiry). 30-day streak ≈ 230 coins ≈ $2.30.
- **Spin → flash conversion:** 1 free spin/day, every spin wins (5% 50% / 10% 30% / 15% 15% / free mystery item 5%), single-use code, 48h, min $25. Expected cost $5.40 per redeemed spin at a $60 order. Store caps: 10 wins/day, $100/week ($50/week first 2 weeks).
- **Referral → new buyers:** friend attends a flash with your code → both get 5% off next pickup, store-funded, opt-in only, 1/customer/store/week.
- **Anti-abuse:** 1 WhatsApp-verified phone = 1 player, 1 device = 1 account, bots/multiple accounts = prize void + account paused, prize pool finite and published, when it is gone the game says so.
- **Legal gate:** SXM prize-promotion / lottery law is unverified. Lawyer check before any game goes live. Games are 18+, under-18s only with a parent/guardian account.
