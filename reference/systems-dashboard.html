Sell on 721 Deals

Two ways to get your dead stock listed — run it yourself, or we come to your store and do it for you.

A — Run it yourself · $0

$0 to start listing

Use the seller dashboard yourself: take your photos, enter sizes, colours, and stock, set your was-price with proof, and publish once approved.

  • Full dashboard access included with your plan
  • You run your own dashboard and your own listings — we do not do it for you. Self-serve stores are not in the done-for-you admin workflow: no store visit, no photos or uploads from us.
  • Outside the done-for-you workflow does not mean outside admin control. Your first 3 listings are admin-approved before they go live — price (minimum 20% off the proven was-price, inside the price floor and ceiling) and photos (real photos, front and back, up to standard).
  • Admin can pause or remove a listing — or the store — if the rules are broken.
  • You control restocks, price changes, and flash slots

B — Done-for-you · we come to your store

$5 per item for founding stores — the first 10 businesses to list

We come to your store, take the photos, gather your inventory, sizes, and colours, and upload everything for you.

  • Founding rate — first 10 businesses: $5 per item — $50 for 10 items · $100 for 20 items · $250 for 50 items
  • Standard rate — after the first 10: $7.50 per item — $75 for 10 items · $150 for 20 items · $375 for 50 items
  • Includes: 1 store visit, photos front + back, size charts, was-price proof, per-size stock upload, live check, and 1 correction round
  • Does not include: restocking, re-photographing sold items, or jewellery/gadget serial verification — serial verification is excluded entirely, it is not sold as an add-on
  • Pro discounts: Pro 6-month pays half price — and it stacks on the founding rate (full table in Pricing below). Pro 12-month is waived — free within the caps: 1 store per day, 20 items per visit. Pro 3-month pays the full done-for-you price. Starter always pays full price — never discounted.

Founding stores: the first 10 businesses get the first 30 days free.

No monthly subscription fee for the first 30 days — you only pay the 10% cut when something actually sells. If it doesn't sell, you pay nothing. When it does sell, the cut comes out of your payout: the buyer's payment is held at Stripe until they confirm pickup, then released to you. And the stock was heading for $0 in storage: $86.80 back beats $0 rotting.

Why 10% on Starter, but 6% on Pro?

Every sale has to cover the same costs — card processing, running the platform, and the marketing that brings buyers to your items. Pro stores pay more up front each month ($150–$175), so we can take less out of each sale. Starter pays less per month ($100), so the per-sale cut is higher to cover the same costs. Either way, the cut only comes out when something actually sells.

  • Pro 12-month ($150/mo) costs $50/month more than Starter but saves 4% on every sale — it pays off once you sell over $1,250/month.
  • Pro 6-month ($175/mo) costs $75/month more — it pays off once you sell over $1,875/month.
  • Below those numbers, Starter is cheaper overall — the lower monthly fee beats the lower cut.
  • On a $100 sale you keep $86.80 on Starter vs $90.80 on Pro, after the $3.20 Stripe fee — try your own numbers in the calculator above.

How the sponsored spaces work

  • Featured deal slot — a store pays to put one of its deals in a promo slot on the page, labelled “Sponsored” so buyers always know it's a paid spot.
  • Top spot in the directory — a store can pay to sit at the top of the store directory for a set period, above the regular order.
  • Flash-window sponsor — a store's deal can lead a flash window (12–2 PM or 7–9 PM) as that window's featured deal.
  • House promos fill the rest — any slot not sold to a store runs 721 Deals' own promos: flash sales, the founding offer, and new-store announcements.
  • Separate from Pro perks: the featured rotation and weekly flash slot included in Pro plans are plan benefits. Sponsored slots are extra, paid placements, open to any store — including Starter stores.
  • Stores only. Sponsored space is sold only to stores selling on 721 Deals. No outside brands, no ad networks, no third-party banners — the page stays clean, like the big marketplaces.

The approval gate — the first 3 listings of every store

No store's first listings go live on trust. For every store — Path A and Path B alike — the first 3 listings are admin-approved before anything is published. Admin checks two things:

  • Price: the deal price is at least 20% off the proven was-price (tag, price list, or dated screenshot), and it sits inside the price floor and ceiling for the category.
  • Photos: real photos of the actual item — front and back — clear and up to the photo standard. No stock photos, no supplier pictures.

A listing that fails either check goes back to the store with the reason — it does not go live half-fixed. After the first 3 listings pass, the store lists freely, and admin spot-checks from then on.

The variant rule — how overselling is prevented.
  • Stock is counted per size × colour, never per product. A total count is not enough.
  • A sold-out size is greyed out and can't be bought — on the picker and on the deal tile.
  • “Continue selling when out of stock” is OFF — always. When the count hits zero, the size stops selling.
